Frequently asked questions

What kind of food does Da Smoke & Spice serve?

A Jamaican food truck menu built around slow-smoked jerk chicken and jerk pork, traditional sides like rice & peas and festival, and homemade island-inspired drinks like ginger drink and island punch. See the full menu.

Does Da Smoke & Spice cater private events?

Yes — catering is available for private events and parties. Start a catering inquiry with your event date and guest count.

How do I get in touch?

Call 301-653-9454, email info@dasmokeandspice.com, or use the catering contact form.
