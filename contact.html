Good to know before you book

Catering lead-time & policies

Booking notice

Drop-off & tray orders under 50 guests: 48–72 hours' notice (rush orders inside 48 hours are subject to availability and a possible rush fee). Large events & full-service spreads of 50–150+ guests: 7–10 days. Major holidays and peak season: 14 days recommended — slots across the DMV corridor fill fast.

Guest count & menu changes

Final guest count and menu selections are due 4 days (96 hours) before your event. We'll try to accommodate guest increases up to 48 hours out, subject to ingredient availability. Decreases after the 4-day deadline can't be refunded, since fresh ingredients and prep are already locked in.

Deposits & payment

Standard orders are paid in full at booking. Large-scale or custom events require a 50% non-refundable deposit to hold your date, with the balance due 48 hours before the event. We accept major credit/debit cards, electronic invoicing, and authorized business accounts.

Cancellations & rescheduling

Cancel 48+ hours out for a full refund (minus any specialty product costs already incurred) or a credit toward a future booking. Cancellations inside 48 hours forfeit full payment, since smoking cycles are already underway. Need to reschedule instead? We'll transfer your deposit to a new date with at least 72 hours' notice, subject to availability.

Delivery & pickup

Delivery and setup are available across the DMV area; fees are based on mileage and event scale from our base kitchen. Free pickup is available at our scheduled staging location — please arrive within a 15-minute window so your food stays at peak temperature. Once handed over, please keep hot-held food safe if your serving window runs past 2 hours.
