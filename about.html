Chef Roger

Tradition is the secret ingredient.

Chef Roger is committed to traditional cooking methods and authentic Jamaican flavors. The process starts with patience — allowing smoke, seasoning and time to work together until every bite carries the character of the island.

Da Smoke & Spice is built around food that feels familiar while still taking you somewhere new: Jamaica, one plate at a time.

Our mission
